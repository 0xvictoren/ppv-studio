This explains what PayPerWatch, run by {{operator}}, collects and why. We keep it to what the service needs to work.

## What we collect

- **Your wallet address.** It identifies you. There are no names, emails or passwords.
- **What you watch and pay for.** Which videos, how many seconds, your signed payment receipts and your watch progress. This is how you're billed once per second, and how creators are paid.
- **What you publish.** Your videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and prices.
- **Technical data.** Your IP address and browser details in our hosting provider's logs, used for security and to limit abuse.

## Public by design

Deposits, payments to creators, withdrawals and video registrations are transactions on the Arc blockchain. Anyone can see them, and they can't be deleted.

## Stored on your device

A sign-in cookie (`ppw_session`) and browser storage for your session key, your referral link and the videos you downloaded. You can clear them in your browser at any time.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your data and there's no advertising tracking. We use service providers to run PayPerWatch: Vercel (hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), Cloudflare R2 (video storage), and your wallet's own providers, such as WalletConnect. They process data only to provide their service.

## Your choices

- Creators can delete their videos from the dashboard. This removes the files and listing from our servers. Records already on the blockchain stay there.
- To ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted from our servers, email {{contact}}.
